**Part-Time Teacher Funding:** "The Foundation provides funds to support a part-time teacher position, helping ensure adequate staffing for the specialized educational needs of transitional students."

**School Supplies & Materials:** "Regular donations of essential school supplies, learning materials, and other needed items ensure students have access to the tools they need for educational success."

- **Flexible Enrollment:** Students can enroll throughout the year as housing situations change  
- **Trauma-Informed Care:** Educational approach that recognizes and responds to trauma impacts  
- **Family Support:** Connection to resources and services for the whole family unit  
- **Extended Hours:** Year-round operation provides consistency when life is uncertain  
- **Basic Needs Support:** Addressing hunger, hygiene, and other fundamental needs  
- **Hope & Stability:** Creating a safe, predictable environment for learning and growth
